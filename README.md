
## Ai - Powered automated waste segregation  and  E waste Recycling systems

EcoTrack SL is a microservice-based AI ecosystem developed as a final year software engineering research project for Sri Lanka.
The platform integrates federated learning, hazard intelligence, contamination detection, recycling optimization, and economic forecasting into one unified smart circular economy system.

---

# Project Overview

The system addresses major e-waste management challenges including:

* lack of district-level forecasting
* poor recycling optimization
* hazardous material handling risks
* inefficient collection processes
* limited economic analytics
* absence of integrated smart waste infrastructure

EcoTrack SL combines multiple AI services into a centralized enterprise platform for government authorities, recycling industries, environmental agencies, and smart city initiatives.

* Docker
* NGINX
